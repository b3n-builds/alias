# ALIAS 1.1.0

Rooms matter now, and everyone has something private to share or bluff about.

- **Pick your room.** Each night a few rooms open, shown during talk. On your secret turn you choose where to spend
  the night. Plan out loud, then go somewhere else if you like.
- **Room powers.** Lookout (learn where one other person was), Locked (nobody can slip out, so no killer can act
  from it), Dark (witnesses can't see out), Crowded (slipping out is twice as likely to be seen).
- **Morning peek.** After the morning report the phone goes round once more. Each player privately sees how many
  were with them and whether someone slipped out of their room. Sometimes also a sound from another room, a trace
  left in the killer's room (only people who were there see it), a lookout sighting, or a rumor that is true 2 times
  in 3. Killers who acted get a cover story to bluff with.
- **Table dares.** One per round, like "On three, everyone points at the person they trust least."
- Rebalanced for all of this with the bot simulator (see BALANCE.md).

## 1.0.4

- New: **Skip vote**. Not sure who it is? Skip. Skip counts like a candidate: if it gets the most votes (or ties for
  most), nobody tops that round and any streak toward being thrown out is broken.

## 1.0.3

- Alibis: everyone is now in a pair or a trio, never all in one place. In 4-player games everyone used to share
  the same place, so a witness report told you nothing.
- The question each round now comes from the evidence: it asks about the place a witness named, or a place where
  people really were last night, instead of a random story question.

## 1.0.2

- Signed with the permanent ALIAS key. From this version on, updates install over the old app.
- **Uninstall any older ALIAS once before installing this one** (older builds used throwaway keys).

## 1.0.1

- Fix: long labels in the top bar no longer push screens wider than the phone.

## 1.0

- 21 cases (17 new), each with its own look, story, names, alibi places, events and questions. Seven are set in Egypt and the Arab world.
- Full Arabic version: switch on the home screen.
- Redesigned as a real mobile game: home screen, 3-step setup, case picker, hold-to-open private screens, tabs during talk, haptics.
- Rebalanced with a bot simulator: killers now have alibis and can be spotted by witnesses, the killer count scales with the table, and difficulty levels mean something. See BALANCE.md.
- The app checks GitHub for new versions and offers the download.

Install: download ALIAS.apk on your Android phone and open it (allow "install unknown apps" for your browser).
If you installed an older build, uninstall it first.
